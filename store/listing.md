## English

**App name (30):** Taekwondo Journey – Kids

**Short description (80):** Learn real taekwondo kicks, belts and poomsae by playing. League & challenges.

**Full description (4000):**

Taekwondo Journey teaches kids real taekwondo, step by step, the fun way.

🥋 **Learn it right**
• Every kick broken into 5 stages with pictures, video and coach tips
• Warm-up before training and cool-down after
• A full belt syllabus from white to black: stances, blocks, kicks and poomsae

🎮 **Play to practise**
• 13+ games for every kick: board breaking, sparring, balance, heavy bag, rhythm and more
• 3 difficulty levels — the game eases off automatically when a child is stuck

🏆 **Compete with friends**
• One daily challenge for everyone
• A weekly league from Bronze to Champions
• 1-vs-1 duels on one screen, and tournaments
• A hero page for every player with achievements and personal records

👩‍🏫 **For coaches and parents**
• A dashboard per player with scores and insights
• PIN-protected coach mode and QR player cards
• Several players on one device, each with their own progress

🔒 **Safe for kids**
• No ads, no purchases, no chat
• No data ever leaves the device
• Works offline

Arabic (Egyptian) and English.
